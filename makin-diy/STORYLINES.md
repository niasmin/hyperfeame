# มากิน มาม่า — DIY clip storylines

Source: IMG_0998.MOV (4:03) + IMG_0999.MOV (4:40), vertical POV — pick pot → fridge toppings → greens → order on tablet → water → noodles → egg/seaweed → seasoning → stir → served.
Assumptions: 9:16, 20–25 s, no voiceover; real cooking sound + effects (music needs a HeyGen sign-in or the shop's own track); real logo on the end card.

## A. ชามของฉัน (recommended — a stranger follows one bowl from empty to done)
- Device / form: the result first, then the steps; object-to-object cuts.
- First 2 s: the finished bowl steaming, noodles lifted on chopsticks; "ทำเองได้ อร่อยกว่า" lands on it, then a rewind whip back to an empty gold pot.
- Beats: hook bowl → rewind → 1 เลือกหม้อ → 2 เลือกท็อปปิ้ง → 3 ผักสด → 4 สั่ง/จ่ายที่แท็บเล็ต → 5 ต้มเอง (water, noodles, egg, seasoning) → stir & serve → logo end card.
- Look: real footage, red #C00000 / mustard #FFD45A / cream from the logo, step numbers in a red circle like the logo's disc.
- Sound: boiling, tongs and wrapper sounds from the footage; a pop on each step number; whoosh on the rewind.

## B. ทั้งหมดในเทคเดียว
- Device / form: one continuous POV take, speed-ramped; no cuts that break the walk.
- First 2 s: hand grabs a gold pot, footage at 8× racing to the fridge.
- Beats: pot → toppings → greens → pay → stove → bowl, each stop slowing to real speed for one second, then ramping on → end card.
- Look: almost no graphics, one small running step counter top-left in the logo red.
- Sound: footage sound only, ramped with the speed; ASMR on the slow stops.

## C. หม้อใบเดียว (bold)
- Device / form: match cuts — the gold pot stays the same circle in the centre while what is inside changes.
- First 2 s: an empty gold pot from above, then hard cuts on the beat fill it: toppings, water, noodles, egg, seasoning.
- Beats: empty pot → 6–8 match-cut fills → steam → pot becomes the logo's red disc → end card.
- Look: pots aligned and scaled to one circle, cream ground at the edges, the logo's disc as the final match.
- Sound: one hit per fill on a steady pulse; a riser into the logo.
